FAQ

Questions from Anand.

Many of our customers are NRI families abroad. Can the website accept their payments?

Yes. Indian payment gateways can accept international cards if your account is approved for them. Your site can also show prices in rupees with a note for overseas buyers, and offer WhatsApp contact that works across time zones.

Can a coaching institute take admission enquiries and show batch timings?

Yes. Course pages can list batches, fees you choose to show and faculty, with an admission enquiry form. With an admin panel you can update batches and post notices yourself.

Can a sweet or food brand ship orders across India?

Yes. An online store can sell packaged products with UPI and card payments, delivery charges by zone and order notifications. FSSAI details you hold can be shown on product pages; shipping is handled through your courier.
