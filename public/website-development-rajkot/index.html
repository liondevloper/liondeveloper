FAQ

Questions from Rajkot.

We make hundreds of part numbers. Can the website handle that?

Yes. Parts can be organised by category, vehicle or machine model, size and material, with a search box. With an admin panel your team can add or edit part numbers from a spreadsheet-style screen.

Can dealers find our products by vehicle or pump model?

Yes. A model-wise filter lets a dealer pick the application and see matching parts, then send an enquiry or WhatsApp message with the part numbers already filled in.

Can the website help with export enquiries?

Yes. An English catalogue with clear specifications, material grades, packaging details and a quotation form makes it easier for overseas buyers to shortlist you. We do not promise orders, but a professional site removes a common reason buyers hesitate.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Lion Digital Solution is an online-first business. Rajkot clients work with us the same way as clients anywhere else: over WhatsApp, phone and video calls, in Gujarati, Hindi or English.
