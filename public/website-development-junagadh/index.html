FAQ

Questions from Junagadh.

We sell Kesar mangoes only for a few weeks a year. Is a website worth it?

Yes, if it is built for the season. The store can open pre-orders before harvest, close automatically when stock runs out and show a "notify me next season" form for the rest of the year, so you keep the customer list you built.

Can the store limit delivery to certain cities?

Yes. Checkout can accept only the pincodes or cities you deliver to, with different delivery charges for each zone. Courier arrangements stay with you.

Can a hotel near Gir take booking requests on the website?

Yes. Guests choose dates, room type and number of guests, and the request reaches you on WhatsApp or email. Safari permits are booked by guests through the official forest department channel; your site can link to it but cannot issue permits.
