Do you work with businesses outside Gujarat?

Yes. Lion Digital Solution is an online-first business based in India. Projects are handled over WhatsApp, phone, email and video calls, so a business in Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru or a smaller town gets exactly the same process.

How much does a website cost in India?

Starter websites begin at ₹15,000, Business websites at ₹25,000 and Professional websites with an admin panel, database and login at ₹40,000. Custom web applications are quoted after a short discussion. The final price is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Can you integrate Indian payment gateways?

Yes. Online stores, booking systems and web apps can use Indian payment gateways that support UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. You open the merchant account with the provider, and we connect it to your website.

Do I get a proper invoice?

Yes, an invoice is provided for every project. Ask about tax invoice details when you request your quote, so the right details are included.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain and hosting are preferably registered under your own account, and the finished website is handed over to you after the final payment.
