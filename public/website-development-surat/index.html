FAQ

Questions from Surat.

Can wholesale buyers see prices only after they log in?

Yes. A wholesale catalogue can show designs to everyone and reveal rates, set quantities and order forms only to approved buyers who log in. This needs a login and admin panel, so it falls in the Professional tier from ₹40,000.

We launch new saree designs every week. Can we upload them ourselves?

Yes. With an admin panel you add photos, design numbers, fabric and price from your phone, and the catalogue updates immediately without calling a developer.

Can a diamond or jewellery website show certified stones?

Yes. Listings can show shape, carat, colour, clarity and the certificate number you provide, with filters for buyers and an enquiry button. Final sale terms stay between you and your buyer.
