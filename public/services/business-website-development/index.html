FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a business website take?

Most business websites are ready in 1–3 days, depending on the number of pages and features. A clear timeline is shared before work begins.

Do you build websites for clients outside India?

Yes. Projects are handled over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and international clients are quoted in US dollars.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every website includes basic SEO: page titles, descriptions, a sitemap, fast loading and mobile-friendly design. Rankings also depend on your competition and content, so no one can honestly guarantee a first-page position.

Can I update the website myself later?

Yes, an admin panel can be added so you can edit content yourself. Changes can also be handled for you through the optional Website Care Plan.
