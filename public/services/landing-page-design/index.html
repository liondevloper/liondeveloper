FAQ

Common questions.

What is the difference between a landing page and a website?

A website explains your whole business across several pages. A landing page is a single page with one goal, such as getting a lead, a booking or a sale, which makes it ideal for ads and campaigns.

Can you connect my landing page to Google Ads or Meta Ads tracking?

Yes. Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tags and the Meta Pixel can be added on request so you can measure results.

How fast can a landing page be ready?

Most landing pages are ready in 1–3 days once the content and offer are clear.
