FAQ

Questions from Vadodara.

Our buyers are large companies and EPC contractors. What should our website show?

Procurement teams look for your product range with specifications, manufacturing capability, quality systems and certifications you hold, a list of industries served and a clear way to request a quotation. Client names and project photos should be included only with permission.

Can you build a quotation request form with drawing uploads?

Yes. Buyers can describe their requirement and attach drawings or specification files. Requests are emailed to you and can also be stored in an admin panel for follow-up.

Can you build internal tools such as job tracking for a workshop?

Yes. Custom web applications for job cards, order status, dispatch records or vendor lists start from ₹40,000. The scope is agreed in writing before work begins.
