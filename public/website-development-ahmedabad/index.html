The Ahmedabad market

Four very different kinds of buyer.

Textiles and garments

Mills, processors and garment makers sell to wholesalers and brands who want to see fabrics, GSM, finishes and minimum order quantities before they call. A catalogue website does that better than a PDF forwarded on WhatsApp.

Pharma, chemicals and dyes

Suppliers in the city's industrial estates are judged on credibility. Buyers look for product grades, certifications, a company profile and a proper enquiry form before adding you to their vendor list.

Startups and IT services

Founders need a sharp landing page to test an idea, and later a working product. We build both: a fast marketing site first, then a web app MVP with login, dashboard and admin panel.

Real estate and interiors

New projects across the city are sold through ads. Each project needs its own landing page with floor plans, amenities and a lead form that reaches the sales team instantly.
