FAQ

Questions from Morbi.

We have hundreds of tile designs. Will the website load slowly?

No. Design images are compressed and loaded as the visitor scrolls, and filters narrow the list before images load. Dealers can browse a large catalogue comfortably on mobile data.

Can buyers filter by size, finish and application?

Yes. Filters can include size such as 600x600 or 600x1200 mm, body type, surface finish, colour, series and application such as floor, wall, outdoor or bathroom.

Can each design have a downloadable high-resolution image or PDF?

Yes. Each design page can offer the face images, technical data and a downloadable catalogue PDF, so architects and dealers can share them with their clients.
