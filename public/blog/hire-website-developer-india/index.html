Hiring a website developer from India.

Many small businesses in the USA, UK, UAE and Australia work with Indian developers to get a professional website at a fair price. Here is how to do it safely and get a result you are happy with.

Lion Digital Solution · Published 28 October 2026

Why businesses hire developers from India

  • Value for money: experienced work at prices that suit small business budgets.
  • English communication: English is widely used in business in India.
  • Overnight progress: the time difference means work can continue while you are offline.

Five things to agree before you start

  1. Scope in writing: pages, features and what is not included.
  2. Price and currency: a fixed quote, for example in US dollars, with no surprises.
  3. Payment schedule: part upfront and the rest on approval protects both sides. We use 50% advance and 50% after you approve the finished website.
  4. Revisions: how many rounds of changes are included.
  5. Ownership: the domain, hosting and code should end up in your name and account.

Red flags to avoid

  • Anyone who guarantees a number one ranking on Google. No one can honestly promise that.
  • Asking for 100% payment before any work is shown.
  • Refusing to register the domain in your name.
  • No live examples of previous work.
  • Vague quotes without a list of what is included.
