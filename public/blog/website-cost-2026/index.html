How much does a website cost in 2026?

The honest answer is "it depends", but that is not useful when you are planning a budget. This guide gives real starting prices, explains what pushes the price up and lists the running costs that are easy to forget.

Lion Digital Solution · Published 28 October 2026

Quick answer

At Lion Digital Solution, a small business website starts from ₹15,000 in India or $399 for international clients. A larger business website with booking or enquiry forms starts from ₹25,000 / $799, and websites with an admin panel, database and logins start from ₹40,000 / $1,299. Prices across the market vary widely, from cheap template sites to agency projects costing many times more.

What makes a website cost more

  • Number of pages. Every page needs its own layout, content and testing.
  • Custom features. Bookings, logins, payments and dashboards take far more work than static pages.
  • Integrations. Payment gateways, maps, CRMs and messaging tools each add setup and testing time.
  • Content. If you need text written or images sourced, that is extra work compared to supplying your own.
  • Revisions. A clear brief upfront keeps revision rounds, and the price, under control.

Costs people forget

  • Domain name: renewed every year. Our Business and Professional plans include a standard .in or .com domain for the first year.
  • Hosting: many small sites can run on free or low-cost hosting; larger apps with databases need paid plans.
  • Maintenance: updates, fixes and small changes. Our optional Website Care Plan starts at ₹999/month or $39/month.
  • Third-party services: payment gateway fees, email services or paid plugins, charged by those providers.

How to get an accurate quote

  1. List the pages you need and the one main action you want visitors to take.
  2. Share two or three websites you like, and say what you like about them.
  3. Mention any must-have features such as bookings, payments or logins.
  4. Ask for the price, timeline, number of revisions and what is not included, in writing.

With that information, we can usually confirm a clear price the same day. See our services or full pricing.
