Landing page vs website: which do you need?

Both live on the internet and both can bring you customers, but they do different jobs. Picking the right one saves money and gets better results.

Lion Digital Solution · Published 28 October 2026

The short version

A website tells the whole story of your business across several pages: who you are, what you offer, your work and how to contact you. A landing page is a single page with one goal, such as getting a lead, a booking or a sale, and it removes everything that distracts from that goal.

Choose a landing page if

  • You are running Google, Facebook or Instagram ads and want a page that matches the ad.
  • You are launching one product, event, course or seasonal offer.
  • You need to collect leads quickly and test an idea on a small budget.

Choose a website if

  • You want to be found on Google for several services or locations.
  • Customers need to see your work, team, prices and reviews before contacting you.
  • You plan to add bookings, an online store or a blog later.

Why many businesses end up with both

A website is your permanent home online, and landing pages are focused entrances for each campaign. Sending ad traffic to a dedicated landing page usually works better than sending it to your homepage, because visitors see exactly the offer they clicked on.
